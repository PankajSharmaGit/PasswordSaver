Privacy Policy · Last updated: 22 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PasswordSaver ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the PasswordSaver mobile application (the "App"). The App is an encrypted password and document vault. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, sign-in method (email/password or Google Sign-In), optional mobile numbers for recovery, and country selection.
  • Vault data you enter: usernames, passwords, service names, notes, and file attachments you choose to store. Passwords and sensitive fields are encrypted on your device before cloud sync.
  • Subscription & payment: plan status, expiry dates, and payment references processed by Razorpay. We do not store full card or UPI credentials on our servers.
  • Device & usage data: device type, OS version, app version, crash logs, and basic analytics to improve reliability.

2. How we use your information

  • Provide vault storage, sync, PIN/biometric unlock, and subscription access.
  • Process payments and activate plans after server-side verification.
  • Authenticate you, prevent abuse, and support account recovery.
  • Show limited advertisements (Google AdMob) where applicable.
  • Improve security, fix bugs, and develop new features.

We do not sell your personal data or vault contents.

3. Encryption & security

PasswordSaver uses AES-256 encryption on-device. Your master encryption key is protected by your app PIN and account credentials. We cannot read your plaintext passwords. The App blocks screenshots and screen recording on supported platforms to reduce accidental exposure.

4. Cloud storage & third-party services

We use trusted providers to operate the App, including:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Storage)
  • Google Sign-In
  • Razorpay (subscription payments)
  • Google AdMob (advertisements)

These services process data only as needed to provide their function. Their use is also governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data sharing

We do not share your vault data with other users. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect users and public safety. If our business is transferred, data may move as part of that transaction with notice where required.

6. Data retention & deletion

We retain account and encrypted vault data while your account is active. You may request account deletion by contacting us; we will remove personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal or tax purposes. Uninstalling the App removes local data on your device but not necessarily cloud backups until deletion is requested.

7. Your choices

  • Update profile and recovery details in the App settings.
  • Control device permissions (biometrics, storage) in system settings.
  • Contact us to request data export or account deletion.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

9. International users

If you select a country outside India, subscription pricing may be shown in USD. Payment processing and data hosting may involve servers in India and other regions used by our cloud providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us
